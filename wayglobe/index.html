How it works

A camera, a globe and your friends. That's it.

Snap a moment

Take a photo in the app or pick one from your library. Wayglobe reads where it was taken.

Choose how exact

Exact spot, nearby (about 1 km) or city only. Near home it's never more exact than nearby.

It lands on their globe

Friends see it pop up where you are, react with an emoji and leave a comment.

A look inside

Your location, your call

A map of your friends shouldn't be a map of where you live.

Three levels of exact

Exact spot, nearby or city only, for every moment. Positions shown to others are snapped to a grid, so repeated posts can't be averaged to find you.

A safe zone around home

Set a home area and anything taken within 600 m of it is never shown more exactly than nearby. Your home area stays on your phone.

Friends first

Moments go to friends by default. Sharing with everyone is something you choose each time.

Reported, reviewed, removed

Report any photo or comment. Content reported by several people is hidden right away while a moderator reviews it, and you can block anyone.

Questions

Is Wayglobe free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Do I need a phone number or email?

No. Pick a name and you're in. Add friends with a 6-letter invite code or by their @name. A recovery code lets you move your account to a new phone.

Can friends see exactly where I am?

Only if you choose Exact spot. You can share a moment as Nearby (about 1 km) or City only, and anything taken near your home area is never shown more exactly than Nearby.

Who sees my photos?

Friends by default. You can also share a moment with everyone on Explore, or keep it private on your own globe.

What goes into a tour video?

Your own moments and friends' moments they shared with everyone. Friends-only moments are never put into a video.

How do you deal with bad content?

Everyone agrees to the community guidelines before posting. Every photo and comment can be reported, content reported by several people is hidden automatically, a moderator reviews reports, and you can block anyone.
