Support

Need help with Lampnote? Email bestappsclub@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. Please include your phone model, system version and the language of the sermon.

Common questions

The transcript is in the wrong language. On the record screen, tap the language button above the red button and pick the sermon's language, or leave it on Auto-detect: Lampnote listens to the first few seconds and picks the language for you.

It says it's downloading a speech model. The first time you use a language your phone downloads its speech model once (it can be a few hundred MB, so Wi-Fi is best). After that it works offline.

No text appears, only audio is recorded. Check Settings › Privacy & Security › Speech Recognition and Microphone, and allow Lampnote. The audio is always saved, so you can still listen later.

A Bible verse wasn't found. Open the sermon, tap Transcript › Fix mistakes and correct the reference (for example “John 3:16”), then save. You can also add verses in your own notes.

Verse text doesn't load. Each verse needs the internet once. After that it's saved on your phone.

Apple Watch can't start a recording. iPhone only lets an app turn on the microphone while it's open. Open Lampnote on your iPhone, then tap Start on the watch. Pause, ★ mark and stop work with the iPhone locked.

How do I get the notes to my small group? Open the sermon and tap the share button: send a card image, a PDF or plain text.
