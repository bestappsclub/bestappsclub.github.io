Privacy Policy

Lampnote: Sermon Notes · iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Android · Last updated 22 November 2026

Lampnote is made by Best Apps Club. In short: we don't collect any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in the app, and we have no server that receives your recordings or notes.

What stays on your device

  • Recordings of sermons you choose to record.
  • Transcripts. Speech is turned into text by the speech recognition built into your device (Apple's on-device speech models on iPhone; the on-device speech recognizer on Android). The first time you use a language, your device may download its speech model from Apple or Google. Your audio is not sent to us.
  • Notes: titles, outlines, key points, questions and highlights. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence these are written by Apple's on-device model; otherwise by simple on-device text processing.
  • What you type: church, speaker, series and your own notes, plus memory verses and settings.

This data is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. If your device backs up apps (for example iCloud Backup or Google backup), it is included in that backup under your own account. Deleting a sermon or the app deletes the data.

What leaves your device

Only to show Bible verse text: when a sermon mentions a verse, the app sends the reference (for example “John 3:16”) and the translation name to a public Bible service, and saves the answer on your device so it isn't requested again:

ServiceUsed for
bible-api.comKJV, World English Bible, Chinese Union Version, Reina-Valera 1909, Korean Revised Version
api.getbible.netArabic Smith & Van Dyck, Bíblia Livre, Louis Segond 1910

No transcript, audio, note or identifier is sent with these requests. Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address when the request is made.

Apple Watch

The watch app talks only to your own iPhone, using Apple's WatchConnectivity. It receives the recording state, your last sermon's key points and your memory verses.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record a sermon, only after you tap record.
  • Speech recognition: to turn the recording into text on the device.

Recording other people

Please respect your church's guidance and local law about recording, and ask the speaker when you are unsure.

Children

Lampnote is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

This website

This website (not the app) uses Google Analytics to count visits, with advertising features and Google signals turned off.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: bestappsclub@gmail.com.
