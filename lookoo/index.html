Questions

Which languages can my child learn?

English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Chinese and Korean.

Is Lookoo free?

You can try 3 snaps free. Lookoo Pro, a subscription, unlocks up to 30 snaps a day, an unlimited Word Box and chats with Snappy. There are no ads.

What happens to the photos?

A photo is only used to name the object in it. There are no ads and no tracking.

What age is it for?

Young children learning their first words in a new language, with a parent setting the daily plan.
