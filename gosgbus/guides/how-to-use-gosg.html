How to use GoSG Bus, in short videos

Arrivals, widgets, leave-now alerts, Ask, Causeway cameras, MRT and traffic, each in a few seconds.

GoSG Bus team · Updated 21 Nov 2026

In this guide
  1. Live arrivals and the bus map
  2. Widgets and the Lock Screen
  3. Leave-now alerts
  4. Ask
  5. Causeway cameras
  6. MRT stations
  7. Traffic on the map

Live arrivals and the bus map

Nearby stops are on the first screen. Tap a stop to open it and see the next three buses of every service, then tap a bus to follow it on the map.

Widgets and the Lock Screen

Add the widget in small, medium or large and pick a stop for each. Open a stop and tap 🔒 to follow it on the Lock Screen.

Leave-now alerts

On Favourites, turn on alerts and pick your bus and your usual times. You can have up to ten, free.

Ask

Type a question in plain words. GoSG answers with buses, where to change and the time door to door. Up to five AI-written answers a day; answers worked out from bus data are unlimited.

Causeway cameras

Open Search to see the Woodlands and Tuas checkpoint cameras before you head to JB.

MRT stations

The MRT tab shows every station and exit. Switch to the list to find a station by name.

Traffic on the map

Traffic is off by default. Open the map, tap the map-style button and turn on Show traffic on map. Accidents and breakdowns appear as pins you can tap.
