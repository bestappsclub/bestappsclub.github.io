How to check the last bus in Singapore

Find first and last bus times for any service at your stop.

GoSG Bus team · Updated 21 Nov 2026

In this guide
  1. Turn on arrival details
  2. Or just ask
  3. Holidays and eves

Turn on arrival details

Go to Settings › Show arrival details. Each service then shows its destination, how often it comes, and first and last bus times for your stop.

Or just ask

Open the Ask tab and type “Last 52 tonight?” or “When's the first 14 tomorrow?”. GoSG answers for the stop you're at or the one you name.

Holidays and eves

Last bus times follow the operator's published timetable. On eves of public holidays some services run later; LTA's live times will show the extra buses when they're on the road.
