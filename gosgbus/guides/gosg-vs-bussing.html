GoSG Bus vs BusSing: which Singapore bus app fits you?

An honest side-by-side, including where BusSing is ahead.

The short version

Both apps show live LTA bus times for every stop, without ads. BusSing is ahead on Apple Watch and offline MRT routing. GoSG is ahead on languages, plain-language trip planning, leave-now alerts and the live bus map with traffic incidents.

Where BusSing is better

If you want your bus on your wrist, BusSing has an Apple Watch app and GoSG doesn't yet. BusSing also plans MRT trips with no signal, which helps underground. It has more ratings on the App Store and ships updates often.

Where GoSG is better

GoSG speaks six languages, so it works for parents and grandparents who read 中文, Melayu or தமிழ். You can type or say a question in plain words and get buses, transfers and time door to door. Leave-now alerts tell you when to walk out for your usual bus. It also runs on iOS 17, so older iPhones are covered.

Which should you try?

Try both on the same commute for a day; both are free to start. If Watch support is a must, pick BusSing. If you want alerts, Ask, or another language, pick GoSG.
