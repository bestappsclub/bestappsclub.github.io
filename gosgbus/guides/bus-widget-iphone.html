How to put Singapore bus times on your iPhone Home Screen

Add the GoSG widget, pick a stop, and track a bus on the Lock Screen.

GoSG Bus team · Updated 21 Nov 2026

In this guide
  1. First, star a stop
  2. Add the widget
  3. Three sizes, any favourite stop
  4. Show only the buses you take
  5. Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
  6. Does it drain the battery?

First, star a stop

Open GoSG, find your stop on Nearby or Search, and tap the ☆. The widget shows your favourite stops, so it stays empty until you have one.

Add the widget

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for GoSG Bus and pick small, medium or large.
  4. Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, and choose which stop it shows.

Widgets and Live Activities arrive in GoSG 2.1.

Three sizes, any favourite stop

Touch and hold the widget to switch between small, medium and large, or tap Edit Widget to pick which favourite stop it shows. Turn on Use Nearest Favourite and it follows you. Tap ↻ for fresh times, or tap the widget to open your favourites.

Show only the buses you take

Open your stop, touch and hold a bus, and tap Show in widget. The widget then lists only the starred buses for that stop. With none starred, it shows them all.

Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

Open a stop and tap 🔒 to follow the whole stop: the next five buses count down on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, even when GoSG is closed. To follow just one bus, touch and hold it and tap Track on Lock Screen, or say “Track my bus with GoSG” to Siri.

Does it drain the battery?

The widget fetches once and then counts down on its own, refreshing every few minutes. It doesn't poll every second.
