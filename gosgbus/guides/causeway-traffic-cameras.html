Check Woodlands and Tuas Causeway traffic before you go

Live checkpoint camera photos, right next to your bus times.

GoSG Bus team · Updated 21 Nov 2026

In this guide
  1. Where to find the cameras
  2. Which cameras
  3. Share with family

Where to find the cameras

Open Search: the checkpoint cameras are at the top. They also appear on bus stops for Causeway services such as the CW buses, 160, 170 and 950.

Which cameras

Woodlands Causeway and the Tuas Second Link, from the public traffic camera feed on data.gov.sg. Photos update every few minutes; pull down to refresh.

Share with family

Tap Share on a camera to send the photo with a caption, so the people waiting for you know what the jam looks like.
