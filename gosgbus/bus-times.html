About these times

Live times come from LTA DataMall, the same feed every Singapore bus app uses, and refresh every 20 seconds while this page is open. First and last bus are from the operators' published timetable for today (weekday, Saturday or Sunday; public holidays follow Sunday). Your location is only used in your browser to find nearby stops.
