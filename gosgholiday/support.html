Common questions

Where do the dates come from? Public holidays from the Ministry of Manpower, school holidays from the Ministry of Education. We check them against both sites when they change.

How do I hide primary or secondary school holidays? Open Settings (the gear at the top) and turn off the lists you don't need.

How do I change reminders? In Settings, choose how many days before you want the first reminder and whether you also want one the evening before.

How do I make the widget show school holidays? Long-press the widget, choose Edit Widget, and pick Primary or Secondary school holidays.

When will 2028 be added? MOM and MOE usually publish the next year's dates in the middle of the year. The app is updated soon after.
