GoSG Holiday

Privacy Policy

GoSG Holiday · iPhone, iPad and Android · Last updated 26 November 2026

GoSG Holiday is made by Best Apps Club. In short: the app collects no data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the app does not connect to any server of ours.

How the app works

Singapore public holidays (from the Ministry of Manpower) and MOE school holidays are built into the app. Countdowns, long weekends and leave tips are worked out on your device.

Settings and reminders

Your choices (which lists to show, when to be reminded) are saved only on your device. Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your device; nothing is sent to us. The app asks for notification permission and works without it.

Calendar and sharing

When you add holidays to your calendar or share a card, the app hands a file to the system share sheet or calendar app that you pick. The app does not read your calendar.

Children

The app is suitable for everyone and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

This website

This website (not the app) uses Google Analytics to count visits, with advertising features and Google signals turned off.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: bestappsclub@gmail.com.
